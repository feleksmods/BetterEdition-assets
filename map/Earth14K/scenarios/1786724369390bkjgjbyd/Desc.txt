The year 1947 marks the dawn of the Cold War and the collapse of old empires. While the United States and the Soviet Union split Europe into rival blocs, the colonial world is beginning to fracture. In Asia, the British Partition gives birth to an independent India and Pakistan, while French Indochina spirals into a brutal guerrilla war between France and the Viet Minh. Across Africa, European empires maintain a fragile grip through massive colonial networks like France's AOF/AEF and Britain's scattered protectorates, but the first sparks of modern nationalism are already starting to ignite.
